Narrated course videos from your slides

Upload a presentation and its slide-by-slide voiceover script. Doctyn checks every slide has narration, records the voiceover, and produces a captioned MP4 — all on your own computer.

1Course files

One presentation and one voiceover script. Uploading replaces the current file of that type; the previous one is kept in input/.replaced/.

Current input files appear here once the engine is connected.

2Produce

Jobs run one at a time on the local engine. Finished work is reused, so re-running a build only redoes what changed.

Validate course

Counts slides, parses every “Slide NN” narration section and checks they match. Free and quick.

Preview voice

Records sample narration for a few slides so you can judge the voice before a full build. Build files are not touched.

Generate course video

Full pipeline: slide images, voiceover per slide, synchronized segments, final MP4, captions, transcript and thumbnail. Can take a long time for large decks.
